Foxgrove Brews

Brew some coffee for the picky local woodland creatures while uncovering their hidden secrets...

About The Game

Inspired by the charm of many cozy game mechanics and the classic Clue board game, Foxgrove Brews is a unique blend of self-paced cafe management, social deduction puzzle-solving, and shop management. In the warm and inviting cafe of Foxgrove Brews, players can craft specialized beverages, befriend quirky and mysterious villagers (each with their own secrets to uncover), and create a space that feels like home.

Key Features

  • ☕ Craft unique beverages with a variety of ingredient options, tailored to each villager's preferences.
  • 🍂 Carry out conversations with unique animal villagers and special visitors to uncover their stories.
  • 🌿 Relaxing zero-rush gameplay without fail states or stressful timers.

Play Our Game Jam Version

Originally created as a submission for Comfy Jam Autumn 2026 where it placed in the top-25 community picks, we decided to build off of player feedback and develop Foxgrove Brews into a fully-expanded game!
