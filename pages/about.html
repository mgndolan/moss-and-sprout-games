About Our Studio

Crafted with Heart & Intention

Founded in Virginia, Moss & Sprout Games LLC is an independent game studio born from a love of slow-paced cozy games that give you a sense of relaxation, wonder, and happiness. We are a small, passionate team who believes in creating games that are not only fun to play, but also provide a safe and welcoming space for all players from any walk of life.

In the crazy world we're living in today, it's important for us to keep the human-ness in what we do while also creating games that are accessible, inclusive, and enjoyable for everyone. Our goal is to create a space where players from all walks of life can take a break from wherever they are in life and travel to peaceful worlds to unwind, create, and explore.
